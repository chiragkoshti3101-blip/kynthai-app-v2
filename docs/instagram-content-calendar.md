## Voice guardrails (HIPAA-safe)
- Trustworthy, transparent, human. Confident about the product; never absolute about health outcomes.
- Never: diagnose, guarantee results, promise medical outcomes, fear-monger, shame.
- Always: "talk to / ask your clinician," "results reviewed by a healthcare professional," honest published pricing, true HIPAA-secure claims.
- No fabricated testimonials — the "customer feature" is anonymized common threads.
- Branded hashtag: `#kynthai`. Fix the brief's `#hippa` typo → `#hipaa`.
