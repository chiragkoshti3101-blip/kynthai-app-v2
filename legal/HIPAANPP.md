## A. Our Pledge Regarding Your Health Data

Kynthai Health Technologies ("Kynthai," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your sensitive health data. This page summarizes our privacy practices. For the complete details of how we handle your information, please read our full Privacy Policy at kynthai.app/privacy.

Kynthai is a consumer health-management platform. **Kynthai is not a HIPAA-covered entity or business associate and does not claim HIPAA compliance.** We follow applicable US federal and state consumer privacy laws, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state consumer health privacy laws.

For questions about our privacy practices, contact our Privacy Officer at **privacy@kynthai.app**.

## B. How We May Use Your Health Data

We use and disclose sensitive health data only for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy or with your explicit consent. These purposes include:

### Providing the Service
We use health data to deliver the features you signed up for, including: medication management and adherence tracking; consultation scheduling with independent doctors and labs on the Kynthai platform; care coordination with designated family members or legally authorized representatives; symptom analysis and AI health insights (advisory only); prescription and lab ordering and result delivery; and appointment and medication reminders.

### Payment and Billing
We use data to process subscription and consultation payments through Stripe, Inc., and to manage billing disputes and refund requests. Card data is handled entirely by our payment processor — we never see or store your full card number.

### Safety and Vital Interests
We may disclose health data to prevent or lessen a serious and imminent threat to health or safety, and to send emergency SOS alerts to the emergency contacts and doctors you have linked to your profile.

### Legal Obligations
We may use or disclose data where required by applicable law, court order, or lawful process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Kynthai, our users, or others.

### Legitimate Interests
We use data for security, fraud prevention, and product improvement, balanced against your privacy rights.

**We never sell your health data, and we never share it for advertising purposes.**

## C. Your Rights Regarding Your Health Data

### 1. Right to Access and Copy
You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your data via Profile → Export Data or by emailing privacy@kynthai.app. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

### 2. Right to Correct
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete health data via your profile settings or by emailing privacy@kynthai.app.

### 3. Right to Delete
You may delete your account and all associated data at any time via Profile → Delete Account. A 7-day cooldown applies before permanent deletion.

### 4. Right to Request Restrictions
You have the right to request restrictions on how your health data is used and shared. Requests must be submitted in writing to privacy@kynthai.app.

### 5. Right to Confidential Communications
You have the right to request that we communicate with you through a confidential channel or at an alternative location. We will accommodate all reasonable requests.

### 6. Right to Withdraw Consent
You may withdraw any consent you have given (including AI data-use consent) at any time. Withdrawal takes effect promptly (typically within 72 hours) and does not affect processing completed before the withdrawal.

### 7. Right to Breach Notification
In the event of a breach of unsecured sensitive health data, we will notify affected individuals and, where required, the Federal Trade Commission and applicable state regulators, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery (consistent with the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule).

## D. Our Data Protection Commitments

- **Purpose limitation:** We use and disclose health data only for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy or with your consent.
- **Data minimization:** We collect only the data necessary to operate the service.
- **Security safeguards:** Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3; uploaded documents and prescription images are additionally encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Access is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, and audit logs record access.
- **No sale, no ads:** We never sell your health data and never use it for advertising.
- **No retaliation:** We will not retaliate against you for exercising your rights or filing a complaint.

## E. Complaints and Grievances

### Complaints to Kynthai
Submit written complaints to our Privacy Officer:
Email: privacy@kynthai.app

Include: (a) your name and contact information; (b) a description of the alleged issue; (c) the date(s) of the issue.

Response Timeline: Acknowledgment within 5 business days. Resolution within 30 calendar days. Complex complaints will be extended with written notice of the reason and expected resolution date.

### Complaints to Regulators
You have the right to file a complaint with applicable regulators:

**Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** — ftc.gov/complaint

**Your state attorney general** (for state privacy law violations)

No retaliation will be taken against you for filing a complaint with Kynthai or with regulators.
